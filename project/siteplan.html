Website Planning Document

Koweh Integrated Services Ltd ICT Centre

Site Name

Koweh Integrated Services Ltd ICT Centre is the registered name of the business the site represents (RC: 7490880). I chose it so that customers who already know the shop, its flyers and its logo recognise the website straight away. The round logo above will appear in the header of every page.

Possible domain (availability not checked): kowehict.com.ng

Site Purpose

The site is an information hub for people in Abuja and Nasarawa State who need printing, design, secretarial or computer training services. It shows what the centre offers, lists the training courses, gives the head office and branch locations, and includes an enquiry form where a visitor can request a service or register for a course.

Planned Pages

  • Home: welcome message, short introduction, service highlights and locations.
  • Services: graphic design and secretarial service lists with a filter.
  • Training: computer, design and web development courses.
  • Contact: enquiry form, addresses and phone numbers.
  • References: sources for any outside images or text, linked in the footer.

Scenarios

  • Do you design and print wedding invitation cards and banners, and how do I place an order?
  • Which computer courses are available, and can I get a certificate at the end?
  • Can you help me with FGN online verification, Remita generation or online registration?
  • Where is your office, and what phone number can I call?

Color Schema

    Page background

    Paragraph text

  • #DCEBE6

    Cards and form backgrounds

Typography

Two fonts, both from Google Fonts, with safe fallbacks.

Poppins Bold

Used for the site name, page headings and buttons. Its round, bold letters match the circular logo.

Open Sans Regular

Used for paragraphs, lists, form labels and navigation because it stays easy to read on small phone screens.

Wireframes

Home page layout in the mobile view and the wider desktop view.
