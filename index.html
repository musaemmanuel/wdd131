Abuja, Nigeria

Abuja is the capital city of Nigeria, located in the Federal Capital Territory (FCT). It became Nigeria's capital in 1991, replacing Lagos. Abuja is known for its beautiful landscapes, modern buildings, well-planned roads, and landmarks such as Aso Rock, the National Mosque, and the National Christian Centre. It is an important centre for government, business, education, and tourism in Nigeria.
